Target Society Sunlit Valley 4.1.5A gameplay parity with the approved Roblox adaptations. Research the assigned source mechanics before implementing. Review findings need evidence, impact, proposed fixes and verification. Record author responses and reviewer rechecks. Mark unavailable reviews pending; do not invent communication or approval.

Keep saves, ownership, inventory and progression authoritative and safe. Preserve existing work. Do not publish the game or enable charges without user instruction. Record research, actual tests, remaining gaps and handoffs. Never upload credentials or unrelated files.
